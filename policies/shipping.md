# Synthetic demo shipping policy

These terms are demonstration content, not a real merchant policy or evidence about any order.

## Shipping and tracking
Demo policy: dispatch normally takes two business days. A tracking number should be provided when a carrier accepts a shipment. A shipping label alone does not confirm dispatch or delivery.

## Delivery confirmation
Demo policy: delivery confirmation requires carrier records for the shipment. An order address or estimated arrival date is not delivery confirmation.

## Missing deliveries
Demo policy: for missing deliveries, review shipping and tracking records with the carrier before deciding whether a replacement or refund is appropriate. Missing tracking alone does not establish non-delivery.
